Go/No-Go Response Inhibition Task

Welcome! This task measures your ability to respond quickly and to inhibit responses when needed.

Instructions:

  • GO Trials: When you see a novel stimulus appear, press the LEFT or RIGHT button/arrow key as quickly as possible (the stimulus will indicate which direction).
  • Response Window: You have up to 500ms to respond. If you don't respond in time, you'll see a timeout message.
  • NO-GO Trials: Sometimes a white circle will appear and turn RED. This is the STOP signal. When you see it turn red, you must NOT press any button/key.
  • The stop signal can appear between 100-450ms after the stimulus.
  • Each stimulus is unique to prevent you from developing task-specific strategies.
  • Mobile Users: Use the LEFT ← and RIGHT → buttons that appear on screen.

Task Structure:

  • Block 1 (Practice): All trials are GO trials to help you learn the task.
  • Block 2 (Experimental): Mix of 30 GO trials and 10 NO-GO trials.
